# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user choice). Deployable by dropping the folder on Netlify, Vercel or GitHub Pages. The catalog lives in one editable file (`js/productos.js`).

## Users

Argentine mate drinkers, mostly from Zona Oeste (GBA) and the brand's Instagram/TikTok audience (~700 followers), browsing on their phone. Two jobs: buying a piece for themselves (a personalized mate, a bag to carry the kit) and buying a gift (Día de la Primavera, Día del Maestro, birthdays).

## Product Purpose

Online storefront for Tienda Mate (@tiendaamatee): a family-run, small-business ("Familia de emprendedores") shop selling mates (imperial de calabaza, laqueados, personalizados/grabados), bombillas de alpaca, canastas 100% cuero, bolsos materos de gamuza, termos and yerba. Visitors browse, add to a cart, enter name + phone, and are sent to the shop's WhatsApp (+54 9 11 6119-5525) with a prefilled message listing name, phone, items and total. There is no online payment. Success = more WhatsApp orders that arrive already complete.

## Positioning

Handmade and personalized: engraved mates, embossed leather baskets with guarda pampa motifs, kits assembled to accompany you everywhere ("Que te acompañe siempre"). Warm, close, joking Rioplatense voice ("Diccionario matero", "Te estamos generando una nueva necesidad").

## Brand commitments (confirmed assets)

- Logo: black line-drawn mate with bombilla + "Tienda Mate" bold rounded sans, on peach (#FDDBB4-ish).
- Bio: "Mates, luego existo... Argentina. Familia de emprendedores".
- Highlights: "Hecho con amor", "El proceso".
- Socials: instagram.com/tiendaamatee, tiktok.com/@tiendaamatee.

## Constraints

- Prices are NOT published anywhere: ship realistic example prices clearly editable in one file; the user will replace them.
- Do not promise shipping or delivery terms; everything is coordinated over WhatsApp.
- No invented testimonials, ratings or sales numbers.
- Voice: Rioplatense Spanish (vos, "cebar", "matear").

## Visual commitment

User chose the category standard: a classic Tienda Nube–style storefront (banner, product grid, side cart), executed at the craft level of the best Tienda Nube / Shopify DTC themes. No conceptual twist; brand lives in the logo's peach, the photography and the copy voice.
